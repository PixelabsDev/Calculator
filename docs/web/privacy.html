Privacy Policy for Pixelabs Web Platform

Effective Date: November 1, 2026  |  Platform: Pixelabs Web (pixelabs.in)  |  Publisher: Pixelabs Digital Services

Privacy Commitment: Pixelabs values user autonomy. We collect only minimal necessary data to operate publishing and platform services, never monetize browsing habits, and maintain strict security standards.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with the Pixelabs Web Platform (pixelabs.in), we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Details: When you register an account, we collect your email address, chosen username, and encrypted password hash.
  • Voluntary Contributions: Articles, comments, feedback, or profile information you choose to submit publicly.
  • Support Communications: Inquiries submitted when contacting support or editorial channels.
  • Technical Logs: Standard request metadata (IP address, user-agent, referrers) processed at the edge by Cloudflare for threat mitigation and caching.

2. Use of Information

We process information strictly for legitimate platform purposes: to maintain and secure accounts, authenticate sessions, prevent abuse, and respond to inquiries.

3. Cookies & Session Management

  • Essential Cookies: Encrypted, HTTP-only, SameSite cookies used exclusively to maintain your login session.
  • Preference Cookies: Remembering your theme preference (Light/Dark).
  • No Tracking Networks: We do not load advertising pixels or behavioral trackers from third-party data brokers.

4. Data Retention & User Rights

You maintain full control over your personal data. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account and personal data at any time by emailing hello@pixelabs.in. Data deletion requests are fulfilled within 30 days.

5. Grievance Redressal (IT Act, 2000 & DPDP Compliance)

In accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, inquiries or grievances may be directed to our compliance team:

  • Entity: Pixelabs Digital Services
  • Designation: Lead Compliance & Grievance Officer
  • Email: hello@pixelabs.in
  • Website: https://pixelabs.in
