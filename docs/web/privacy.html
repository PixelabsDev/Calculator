Privacy Policy for Pixelabs Web Platform

Effective Date: November 1, 2026  |  Platform: Pixelabs Web (pixelabs.in)  |  Entity: Pixelabs Digital Services / Vishal Kumar

Privacy Commitment: Pixelabs values user autonomy. We collect only minimal necessary data to operate our publishing and web platform services, never monetize user browsing habits, and uphold strict data security standards.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with the Pixelabs Web Platform (pixelabs.in), we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information: If you register an account, we collect your email address, chosen username, and encrypted password hash.
  • Voluntary Profile & Content Contributions: Any biography details, avatar links, articles, comments, or feedback you choose to publish publicly.
  • Contact Communications: Records of your correspondence when you contact support or submit editorial queries.
  • Technical Logs: Non-personal technical metadata such as browser user-agent, operating system, IP address, and referrers collected automatically by edge CDNs (Cloudflare) for threat mitigation and caching.

2. Use of Information

We process your information strictly for legitimate platform purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and secure user accounts and publishing services.
  • To authenticate sessions and prevent unauthorized access.
  • To respond to user inquiries, technical feedback, or support tickets.
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent activity, spam, and abuse.

3. Cookies & Session Management

Pixelabs uses cookies strictly for essential platform functionality:

  • Essential Session Cookies: Encrypted, HTTP-only, SameSite cookies used exclusively to keep you authenticated while logged in.
  • Preference Cookies: Saving your UI theme preference (Light/Dark).
  • Zero Third-Party Advertising Trackers: We do not load advertising beacons, tracking pixels, or cross-site fingerprinting scripts from data brokers.

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We disclose information only under specific, limited conditions:

  • Legal Mandates: When required by applicable law, court order, or governmental regulation under the Information Technology Act, 2000.
  • Safety & Protection: To protect the rights, property, or safety of Pixelabs, our users, or the public from imminent harm or cyber threats.
  • Infrastructure Service Providers: Trusted cloud infrastructure vendors (e.g., DNS, CDN, and hosting providers) bound by strict confidentiality obligations.

5. Data Retention & User Rights

You maintain full control over your personal information:

  • Access & Correction: You may inspect and update your account details at any time via your account settings.
  • Data Deletion: You may request the deletion of your account and associated personal data by emailing hello@pixelabs.in. Once confirmed, all account data is permanently deleted from our active databases within 30 days.

6. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard technical safeguards to protect your personal data, including strict HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption across all web traffic, modern cryptographic password hashing (Argon2 / bcrypt), and role-based access controls for internal systems.

7. Grievance Redressal (IT Act, 2000 & DPDP Compliance)

In accordance with the Information Technology Act 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, the contact details of the Grievance Officer for Pixelabs are provided below:

  • Grievance Officer: Vishal Kumar
  • Organization: Pixelabs Digital Services
  • Designation: Lead Compliance & Privacy Officer
  • Email: hello@pixelabs.in
  • Official Website: https://pixelabs.in

All grievances received will be acknowledged within 48 hours and resolved in accordance with statutory requirements.
