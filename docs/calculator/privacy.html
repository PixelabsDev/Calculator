Privacy Policy for Pixelabs Calculator

Effective Date: November 1, 2026  |  Application: Pixelabs Calculator for Android  |  Publisher: Pixelabs Digital Services / Vishal Kumar

Core Privacy Guarantee: Pixelabs Calculator is built as a 100% offline utility. We do not track, collect, profile, sell, or transmit your personal data, calculation inputs, or device identifiers to any remote server or third party.

1. Overview and Philosophy

Pixelabs Digital Services ("Pixelabs", "we", "us", or "our") develops software adhering to the principle of Privacy by Design. Pixelabs Calculator for Android is engineered to function entirely on your device without requiring an active internet connection, user accounts, or intrusive device permissions.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

To provide absolute transparency for users and app store review authorities (including Google Play Store Policy verification):

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or government identification.
  • No Mathematical Input or Result Logging: Your formulas, numbers, trigonometric values, memory operations, and history remain exclusively on your device. They are never transmitted over the network.
  • No Unique Device Identifiers: We do not collect, read, or hash IMEI numbers, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), MAC addresses, or hardware serial numbers.
  • No Location Data: We do not access GPS, Wi-Fi triangulation, cellular cell ID, or IP-based geolocation.
  • No Third-Party Analytics SDKs: We do not bundle Google Firebase Analytics, Facebook Audience Network, AppsFlyer, Adjust, or any advertising/behavioral tracking SDKs.

3. Local Device Storage (On-Device Only)

The application utilizes standard, sandboxed Android local storage solely to deliver intended utility features:

  • Calculation History: Previous calculations are saved locally using an on-device Android Room SQLite database. This data is stored strictly within the private application sandbox and is accessible only to you through the app's History drawer. You can clear this history at any time with a single tap.
  • User Preferences: Settings such as selected theme (Light, Dark, System Default) and angular mode (Degrees or Radians) are stored locally using Android Jetpack DataStore (Preferences).

This data never leaves your physical device and is permanently removed when you uninstall the application or clear application data via Android OS Settings.

4. Optional Voluntary Feedback

If you choose to submit voluntary feedback or report a bug within the application:

  • Submissions require an explicit, deliberate user action (clicking a "Submit Feedback" or "Send Report" button).
  • Feedback data is transmitted securely via HTTPS directly to our designated support queue.
  • Submissions may include technical diagnostics you explicitly review (e.g., app version, Android OS version, and voluntary comments).
  • Feedback data is retained for a maximum of 90 days solely for debugging and customer service resolution, after which it is permanently purged.

5. Device Permissions

Pixelabs Calculator requests only minimal or zero sensitive Android OS runtime permissions. It does NOT request access to:

  • Contacts, Calendar, or Accounts
  • Camera, Microphone, or Audio Sensors
  • Shared External Storage or Photos/Media files
  • Precise or Approximate Geolocation
  • SMS, Telephony, or Call Logs

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Pixelabs Calculator does not target children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area), nor does it knowingly collect any information from children. Because the application operates completely offline without tracking, it is fully safe for educational and general audience use in accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Security Architecture

All data processed by Pixelabs Calculator remains sandboxed within Android's process boundary, protected by Linux-level UID isolation and Android system-level encryption (File-Based Encryption). No network attack surface exists for the offline computation engine.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new app features, legal compliance requirements, or platform updates. Any changes will be published here with an updated "Effective Date". Because the app does not collect user contact details, we encourage users to review this page periodically.
