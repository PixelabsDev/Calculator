Privacy Policy for Pixelabs Calculator

Effective Date: November 1, 2026  |  Application: Pixelabs Calculator for Android  |  Publisher: Pixelabs Digital Services

Privacy Guarantee: Pixelabs Calculator is built as a 100% offline utility. We do not track, collect, profile, sell, or transmit personal data, calculation inputs, or device identifiers to any remote server or third party.

1. Overview

Pixelabs Digital Services ("Pixelabs", "we", "us", or "our") develops software with Privacy by Design. Pixelabs Calculator for Android functions entirely on your device without requiring an internet connection, accounts, or intrusive permissions.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

To provide full transparency for users and app store review authorities (including Google Play Store Policy verification):

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or addresses.
  • No Calculation Logging: Your mathematical expressions, numbers, trigonometric values, memory states, and history remain exclusively on your device. They are never transmitted over the network.
  • No Unique Device Identifiers: We do not collect, read, or hash Android Advertising IDs (AAID), IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, or serial numbers.
  • No Location Data: We do not access GPS, Wi-Fi triangulation, or IP geolocation.
  • No Third-Party Analytics SDKs: We do not bundle Firebase Analytics, Facebook Audience Network, or any advertising/behavioral tracking SDKs.

3. Local Device Storage

The application uses standard Android sandboxed local storage solely to provide core offline features:

  • Calculation History: Previous calculations are saved locally using an on-device Android Room SQLite database. This data is stored strictly in the private application sandbox and is accessible only to you. You can clear your history at any time.
  • User Preferences: Settings such as selected theme (Light, Dark, System Default) and angular mode (Degrees or Radians) are stored locally using Android Jetpack DataStore.

This data never leaves your device and is permanently removed when you uninstall the application or clear application data via Android settings.

4. Optional Voluntary Feedback

If you choose to submit voluntary feedback or report a bug within the application:

  • Submissions require an explicit, affirmative user action (tapping a submit button).
  • Data is transmitted securely via HTTPS directly to our designated support queue.
  • Submissions may include technical diagnostics you review (e.g., app version, Android OS version).
  • Feedback data is retained for a maximum of 90 days solely for debugging and customer service, after which it is permanently purged.

5. Permissions

Pixelabs Calculator requests zero sensitive Android runtime permissions. It does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone, location, shared external storage, or telephony.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Pixelabs Calculator does not target children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area), nor does it collect information from children. Operating completely offline without tracking, it is fully safe for educational and general audience use in compliance with COPPA and GDPR.

7. Security Architecture

All data processed by Pixelabs Calculator remains sandboxed within Android's process boundary, protected by Linux-level UID isolation and Android system-level encryption (File-Based Encryption). No network attack surface exists for the offline computation engine.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be published here with an updated effective date.
