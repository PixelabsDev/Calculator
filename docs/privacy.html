Pixelabs Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026  |  Published by Pixelabs Digital Services

Summary: Pixelabs mobile applications, including Calculator for Android, are engineered to operate 100% offline. We do not collect, track, store, profile, or sell any personal data, calculation inputs, or device identifiers.

1. Introduction

Pixelabs Digital Services ("Pixelabs", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting user privacy through technical architecture. This Privacy Policy explains our data safety standards, local device storage practices, and offline computing guarantees across our mobile applications, including Pixelabs Calculator for Android.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

In accordance with Google Play Store User Data policies and international privacy regulations, our applications do not collect or transmit:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or billing credentials.
  • User Calculations & Inputs: All equations, arithmetic operations, scientific calculations, and numerical inputs remain strictly in your device's local memory and are never transmitted to any server.
  • Hardware & Device Identifiers: We do not read, hash, or transmit Android Advertising IDs (AAID), IMEI numbers, MAC addresses, or serial numbers.
  • Location Data: We do not request, access, or infer your precise or approximate geographical location.
  • Advertising & Behavioral Trackers: We do not include third-party advertising networks, user-profiling SDKs, or behavioral analytics libraries.

3. Local Device Storage

Our applications store data exclusively on your physical device within sandboxed local storage:

  • Calculation History: Previous calculations are saved locally using an on-device Android Room SQLite database. This history is accessible only on your device and can be cleared at any time with a single tap.
  • User Preferences: Display options (such as dark mode and angular units DEG/RAD) are saved locally using Android Jetpack DataStore.

This data never leaves your device and is permanently deleted if you uninstall the application or clear app data via Android system settings.

4. Voluntary Feedback & Diagnostics

If you choose to submit feedback or report an error within an application:

  • Feedback submission is strictly voluntary and requires an explicit tap on a submit button.
  • Transmissions are encrypted using HTTPS directly to our support queue.
  • Feedback may include diagnostic metadata you review (such as app version and Android OS level).
  • Feedback records are retained for a maximum of 90 days solely for customer support and troubleshooting, after which they are permanently deleted.

5. Permissions

Pixelabs applications request zero or minimal Android permissions. We do not access sensitive permissions such as contacts, microphone, camera, SMS, location, or shared external storage.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

Because our applications operate completely offline and collect zero personal data, they are safe for users of all ages, including children under 13, in full compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR-K.

7. Security Architecture

All application code runs within standard Android application sandboxes, protected by Linux user-level process isolation and Android File-Based Encryption. There is no remote network attack surface for offline calculation operations.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be published at this static URL with an updated effective date.
