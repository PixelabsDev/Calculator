Privacy Policy for Calculator

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 • Application: Pixelabs Calculator for Android

Core Privacy Guarantee: Pixelabs Calculator is built as a strictly offline utility. It does not track, collect, monitor, or transmit your mathematical expressions, calculation history, or device identifiers to any remote servers.

1. Core Functionality & Offline Operation

The Application is designed primarily as a local utility tool. All arithmetic, scientific functions, trigonometry calculations, and formula evaluations are processed 100% locally on your device's hardware using deterministic on-device parsing algorithms.

  • No Internet Permission for Core Math: Standard calculations require zero network access.
  • Local Storage Isolation: Calculation history entries and user preference themes (System, Dark, Light) are stored strictly inside your device's private sandboxed application storage (via Android SharedPreferences/DataStore).
  • Local Data Control: Users can wipe or clear past calculation history at any moment using the in-app history trash action with instant local erasure.

2. User Feedback & Voluntary Submissions

The Application connects to the internet only and exclusively when a user voluntarily and deliberately chooses to submit feedback or report an issue via the in-app "Send Feedback" modal dialog.

Submitting feedback is entirely optional. When you choose to submit feedback, the following user-provided data is transmitted with your explicit opt-in consent:

  • Feedback Content: Category (Bug Report, Calculation, Suggestion, General), subject, and description provided by the user.
  • Email Address (Optional): Only if voluntarily provided by the user for follow-up communications. You are free to leave this field blank.
  • System Diagnostics (Optional): Non-identifiable technical diagnostics (such as Android OS version and device manufacturer/model) solely to diagnose hardware-specific layout or calculation defects, collected only if the diagnostic checkbox is explicitly enabled by the user.
  • Device Integrity Checks: On-device security audits (such as Google Play Integrity verification) are evaluated locally on your device for user awareness and are not transmitted unless explicitly included by the user in a diagnostic report.

3. Data Retention & Deletion Policy

  • 90-Day Automatic Purge: All submitted voluntary feedback entries, including any accompanying diagnostic details and optional email addresses, are stored securely and automatically purged/permanently deleted within 90 days of receipt.
  • Anonymous Submissions: Feedback submitted without an email address is entirely anonymous and contains zero personal identifiers. Because anonymous entries cannot be cryptographically or technically linked to a specific user, individual anonymous submissions cannot be selectively deleted upon request.
  • Identifiable Data Requests: If you provided an email address during feedback submission and wish to request manual removal of your feedback prior to the 90-day automatic deletion window, you may contact us at hello@pixelabs.in from that specific email address.

4. Permissions & Third-Party Trackers

  • No Sensitive Permissions: The Application does not request or require sensitive Android permissions such as Location, Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Phone State, or External Storage.
  • Zero Analytics & Zero Ad SDKs: The Application contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero user tracking libraries (e.g., no Firebase Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no Adjust, no AppsFlyer), and zero automated background telemetry.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in applicable European jurisdictions). The core mathematical utility is completely safe for users of all ages, and feedback submissions require voluntary adult oversight.

6. Security & Data Protection

Any voluntary feedback submitted is transmitted using modern cryptographic protocols (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). We enforce strict security controls to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure of voluntary reports.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect regulatory standards or new app enhancements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date. Continued use of the Application signifies acceptance of any updated terms.
