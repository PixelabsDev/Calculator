Privacy Policy for PyPad

Effective Date: November 1, 2026  |  Application: PyPad  |  Publisher: Pixelabs Digital Services

Local-First Guarantee: PyPad executes Python code locally on your device within an isolated sandbox. Your scripts, data files, and console outputs are never transmitted to any external server.

1. Overview

PyPad is developed by Pixelabs Digital Services ("Pixelabs", "we", "us") as a private, offline development environment. We operate under strict Privacy by Design principles.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No Script or Code Logging: Code you write, execute, or debug remains strictly on your device. We do not read, transmit, or analyze your Python scripts.
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not track Android Advertising IDs (AAID), IMEI numbers, or hardware identifiers.
  • No Third-Party Analytics: PyPad contains no advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or telemetry beacons.

3. Local Storage

All scripts, notebooks, project files, and application settings are stored strictly within the application's isolated local sandbox. You can export, back up, or permanently delete your files at any time.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Because PyPad operates offline with zero tracking or data collection, it is safe for educational, school, and academic environments without age-restricted data risks.
